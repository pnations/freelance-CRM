# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, professional, and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Use inclusive language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Sharing others’ private information without consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue marked "conduct" or contact the maintainers privately. We will review reports promptly and act to maintain a safe environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action, including warnings, temporary bans, or removal from the community spaces.
